1. SUMMARY & TYPE:
   [Bug Fix | Idempotent Transport Connection] Avoid duplicate ConnectTransport on renegotiated publisher offers

2. REASON & TECHNICAL EVIDENCE:
   - When a participant connects to a room:
     1. Initial offer `offer_id: 1` (DataChannel only) calls `PublishTracks`, which calls `a.transportPort.ConnectTransport(...)` to establish the WebRTC DTLS handshake on `PublisherTransportID`.
     2. In `sfu-media-node` (Rust / mediasoup), `WebRtcTransport::connect` succeeds and marks the transport as connected.
     3. Next, the participant calls `publishTracks({ audio: true, video: true })`, sending `SignalRequest_Offer` with `offer_id: 2` containing the new audio/video media descriptions.

        ```go
        // 1. Connect Publisher Transport on the SFU
        err := a.transportPort.ConnectTransport(ctx, &domain.ConnectTransportCommand{
            RouterID:           a.room.RouterID,
            TransportID:        p.PublisherTransportID,
            DtlsRole:           dtlsRole,
        ```
